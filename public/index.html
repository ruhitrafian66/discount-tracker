Shops

Regular prices (Kassalapp)

Flyers only show a regular price on about 30% of offers. With a free Kassalapp API key, the app also looks up each chain's shelf price. That gives more offers a reference price, and lets the app drop "deals" that are dearer than another shop's normal price.
